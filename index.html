Common Issues

Experiencing Any of These?

If something doesn't feel right with your vehicle, don't ignore it. These are common signs that your car needs professional attention.

Warning Light on Dashboard

Engine, ABS, or service lights can indicate critical issues that need proper diagnosis.

Strange Engine Noise

Knocking, ticking or rattling sounds often signal internal engine issues requiring inspection.

Get Diagnosed →

Brake Vibration or Squeal

Worn pads, warped discs or caliper issues can compromise your stopping power.

Get Diagnosed →

Suspension Knocking

Clunks over bumps usually mean worn bushings, drop links, or shock absorbers.

Get Diagnosed →

Vehicle Losing Power

Loss of acceleration can be fuel, ignition, turbo or sensor related — diagnostics will identify the cause.

Get Diagnosed →

Electrical Fault

Flickering lights, dead batteries, or malfunctioning systems need systematic fault tracing.
